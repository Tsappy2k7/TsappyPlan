TsappyPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Introduction

TsappyPlan is a student productivity application designed to help users manage tasks, assignments, examinations and study schedules.

2. Information We Store

TsappyPlan may store:

  • Tasks and due dates.
  • Assignment details.
  • Examination schedules.
  • Study timetable information.
  • App preferences.

3. How Information Is Stored

Planner information is stored locally on your device using browser storage. TsappyPlan does not currently require accounts, passwords or cloud synchronisation.

4. Sharing of Information

The current version of TsappyPlan does not send your planner information to TsappyPlan servers or share it with third parties.

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when you visit the website.

5. Backups

Users can export and import planner information. Exported backup files are controlled by the user.

6. Data Deletion

Users can remove locally stored planner information by clearing the relevant browser or device data. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data may also remove this information.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as TsappyPlan develops and introduces new features.
